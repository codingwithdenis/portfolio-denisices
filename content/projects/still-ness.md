      **Premise.** Still / Ness is a study in patience. Each shot is a single
      fixed-frame take lasting between 4 and 12 minutes, capturing the slowest
      visible transitions in coastal light. The title is a double meaning —
      "stillness" as in absence of motion, and "still" as in the photographic
      frame.
