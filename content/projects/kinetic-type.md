  weight, and timing.
blocks:
  - type: text
    body: |
      **Concept.** Type is usually read, not seen. Kinetic Type strips away
      legibility and asks the viewer to experience letterforms as physical objects
      — weight, surface, momentum. Each character was modelled in 3D and assigned
      unique material properties before being released into a simulated environment.

      **Process.** Built with Three.js and a custom Verlet physics engine. Each
      typeface was dissected into individual glyphs, re-extruded as 3D geometry,
      and animated through a system of constraints — spring forces, collision
      boundaries, and turbulence fields. The music track was generated from the
      vertex positions via MIDI mapping.
